Equation 1: Hat shell energy balance
\[ lpha f_{view} G + h\,(T_a - T_s) + arepsilon\sigma\,(T_{sky}^4 - T_s^4) = 0 \]

For a round hat, \(f_{view}\) accounts for curved geometry reducing effective direct solar load.

Equation 2: Equivalent cooling metric
\[ q_{reduction} = (lpha_{base} - lpha_{ant})\,f_{view}\,G \]
\[ E_{equiv} = rac{q_{reduction}}{COP}\cdot rac{hours}{1000} \]

This converts heat-flux reduction to an equivalent cooling-electricity intensity basis for comparison.
