Equation 1: Roof surface steady-state energy balance
\[ \alpha G + h\,(T_a - T_s) + \varepsilon\sigma\,(T_{sky}^4 - T_{s}^4) = 0 \]

where:
\(\alpha\) = solar absorptance, \(G\) = solar irradiance (W/m²), \(h\) = convection coefficient (W/m²K),
\(\varepsilon\) = IR emissivity, \(\sigma\) = Stefan-Boltzmann constant, \(T_a\), \(T_s\), \(T_{sky}\) in Kelvin.

Equation 2: Cooling load reduction at roof-to-indoor boundary
\[ q_{in} = \max\left(0, U\,(T_s - T_{in})\right) \]
\[ q_{saved} = q_{in,baseline} - q_{in,ant} \]
\[ E_{saved} = \frac{q_{saved}}{COP}\cdot\frac{hours}{1000} \]

where \(U\) is roof U-value (W/m²K), \(T_{in}\) indoor setpoint, and \(COP\) cooling system coefficient of performance.
